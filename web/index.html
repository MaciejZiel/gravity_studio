Gravity Studio
---- Live

Solar System

Sizes are enlarged and distances compressed so every body stays visible. True distances switches distances to linear au.

WebGL is not available

This viewer draws the Solar System with WebGL, and your browser or device did not provide a WebGL context.

  • Turn on hardware acceleration in the browser settings and reload.
  • Update the browser and the graphics driver.
  • Try another browser, for example a current Firefox, Chrome, Edge or Safari.

The desktop version of Gravity Studio does not need WebGL.

Credits

Planet textures (Mercury to Neptune, Earth night lights and clouds, Saturn rings) come from Solar System Scope and are licensed under CC BY 4.0. The files used here are unmodified 2k maps.

Planet positions use the approximate Keplerian elements of E. M. Standish and J. G. Williams, published by the JPL Solar System Dynamics group.

The Sun and moon surfaces are generated procedurally in your browser. 3D rendering by three.js (MIT).

Moons other than Earth's Moon use circular orbits with arbitrary starting phases. Mission paths are schematic.
